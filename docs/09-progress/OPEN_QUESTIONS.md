# Open questions

**2026-09-23 product update:** The [workflow plan](OPERATOR_WORKFLOW_IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md)
records Heating/Hold-only authoring, automatic cooling and Manual cooling at
48 hours. These resolve corresponding product choices below, pending normative
baseline reconciliation. Endpoint/estimation, pause/deadline semantics and
integration remain open in the [controller backlog](CONTROLLER_INTEGRATION_BACKLOG.md).
Older six-hour/authored-COOL text is not the new requested behavior.

These unresolved items do not block repository foundation work. They must not
be answered implicitly by placeholders, examples, or simulator behavior.

Reviewed 2026-09-02. The foundation and first visual slices intentionally did
not resolve the operational and hardware decisions below.

| Hot start | Resolved 2026-10-16: inclusive 5 C Hold entry tolerance, configurable per model; no normal confirmation timeout; full Hold duration after a hot start. Still open: link-liveness threshold and temperature reporting interval, fallback-timeout value where liveness cannot be assured, hot starts in Manual mode. | Furnace START-03, START-05, START-09, START-11; [ADR-0014](../07-decisions/0014-live-hot-start-confirmation-and-sensor-faults.md) |
| Leading Hold | This repository previews a first-stage HOLD as a delayed start; the furnace requires the first stage to be Heating (furnace PROG-02, HMI-DIFF-03). Which applies? | Product owner; furnace requirements |
| Cooling estimate | What data and reliability criterion would an informational cooling-time estimate use, and who computes it? | Furnace START-08; CTRL-COOLING |
| Hold qualification | Resolved 2026-10-18: a Hold's treatment timer starts only after the temperature is in the treatment band and has met the stability qualification; no credit before it; full duration after a hot start. Resolved 2026-10-20: an interruption keeps the credited time and requalifies before crediting resumes. Still open: treatment band and stability interval values, filtering and hysteresis, and limits on interruption duration or magnitude with their response (invalidate, restart the Hold or terminate). | Furnace PROF-03, PROF-09 to PROF-14; [ADR-0015](../07-decisions/0015-hold-qualification-and-treatment-time.md), [ADR-0016](../07-decisions/0016-program-execution-presentation.md) |
| Run execution | Resolved 2026-10-20: temperature-driven Heating completion, ramp holdback, already reached Heating stages, operator Pause and explicit Resume, distinct outcomes, curing never shown as accepted without an acceptance policy. Still open: controlled stopping and cooling, fan behavior during Pause (provisional: circulation above a threshold), the process-acceptance policy, who may Resume, Stop or acknowledge a deviated run, and all threshold values. | Furnace RUN-04, RUN-07 to RUN-11, FAN-04, PROF-13; [ADR-0016](../07-decisions/0016-program-execution-presentation.md) |
| Fault classes and diagnostics | Resolved 2026-10-22: recoverable faults pause with explicit Resume; critical faults and protective trips latch, refuse Resume and need Stop; acknowledging never restarts heating; transition deviations are reported, not smoothed; control diagnostics belong in a service view ([ADR-0017](../07-decisions/0017-fault-classes-and-control-diagnostics-presentation.md)). Still open: which fault reasons are critical per model and process; the recovery or service procedure for a latched fault and who may perform it; permissions and units for the diagnostics view; whether a large transition deviation should also pause the run. | Product owner, Control CPU |
| Fault manager and Hold approach | Resolved 2026-10-22: one controller fault manager owns classification, latches, acknowledgement and history; acknowledgement, recovery and Resume are separate; a required-sensor failure is critical; the Hold setpoint approaches its target at the ramp rate ([ADR-0018](../07-decisions/0018-fault-manager-and-hold-approach-presentation.md)). Still open: approved recovery procedures and roles for latched faults; whether latches survive a controller restart; fault-history persistence and query; per-model classification. | Product owner, Control CPU |
| Duration, quality decisions and runtime | Resolved 2026-10-23: no roles; quality-only faults may continue after an explicit decision during a bounded wait that holds the setpoint; equipment and safety faults never; advisory durations are acknowledged, the maximum is never overridable, accepted programs run their full schedule and a hard runtime limit stops a run that cannot complete; chamber temperature is the process measurement; completion is not a verified cure ([ADR-0019](../07-decisions/0019-duration-decisions-and-runtime-presentation.md)). Still open: recommended-action code table; decision-wait and runtime values per model; Save-time acknowledgement with a controller program store; fault persistence across restarts. | Product owner, Control CPU |
