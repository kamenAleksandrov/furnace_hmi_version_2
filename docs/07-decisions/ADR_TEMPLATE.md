# ADR-NNNN: Short decision title

- Status: Proposed
- Date: YYYY-MM-DD
- Owners: roles or team
- Governing requirements: links/IDs

## Context

State the problem, current facts, constraints, assumptions, and evidence. Keep
facts separate from recommendations. Explain why a decision is needed now.

## Decision drivers

- Observable criterion.

## Options considered

Describe viable alternatives and the strongest reasonable case for each. Do
not invent a strawman to justify the preferred option.

## Decision

State exactly what is selected and what remains open. A Proposed or Deferred
record must label this section as a proposal or decision to defer.

## Consequences

Record benefits, costs, risks, migration/reversibility, and operational or test
implications.

## Verification and follow-up

List evidence required to accept and checks/documents that must change.

## Review record

Summarize domain-review and decision-challenger findings, including unresolved
objections. Link durable review artifacts where available.
